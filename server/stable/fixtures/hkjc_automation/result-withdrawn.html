Racing Incident Report

Pla. Horse No. Horse Incident
1 7 LIGHT YEARS CHARM (K130) Sent for sampling post-race.
2 6 PATCH OF THETA (H485) Sent for sampling post-race.
3 2 MY WISH (J256) Bumped shortly after the start.
4 10 SKY JEWELLERY (K032) Jumped only fairly. Shortly after the start was steadied when crowded between MY WISH and SOLEIL FIGHTER, both of which shifted ground. Sent for sampling post-race.
5 5 BEAUTY JOY (E058) From a wide barrier was shifted across behind runners in the early stages. After passing the winning post was checked when crowded between SKY JEWELLERY (A Badel) and PATCH OF THETA (M F Poon), both of which shifted ground. Both A Badel and M F Poon were advised to exercise more care.
6 3 HELIOS EXPRESS (H302) On jumping was bumped by WINNING WING which shifted out.
7 9 SOLEIL FIGHTER (J127) No report.
8 12 WINNING WING (K129) Shifted out at the start and was bumped.
9 8 STORMY GROVE (K478) K C Leung could offer no excuse for his mount’s disappointing performance. A veterinary inspection immediately following the race did not show any significant findings.
10 11 SAGACIOUS LIFE (K507) Raced keenly in the early stages. E C W Wong reported that his mount made a respiratory noise during the race. A veterinary inspection immediately following the race found that horse to be a "roarer” which had previously been reported.
11 1 VOYAGE BUBBLE (E436) C L Chau stated that his mount failed to respond to his riding in the Home Straight and the only explanation he could offer was that VOYAGE BUBBLE did not appreciate carrying top weight of 135 lbs and being first up today. Trainer P F Yiu stated that the horse had pleased him leading into the race and concurred with the explanation offered by Jockey Chau. A veterinary inspection immediately following the race did not show any significant findings. The performance of VOYAGE BUBBLE was considered disappointing. Before being allowed to race again, VOYAGE BUBBLE will be required to perform to the satisfaction of the Stewards in a barrier trial and be subjected to an official veterinary examination.
WV 4 INVINCIBLE IBIS (K296) Withdrawn on 15.10.26 by order of the Stewards acting on veterinary advice (left front ligament injury). Before being allowed to race again, INVINCIBLE IBIS will be subjected to an official veterinary examination.
